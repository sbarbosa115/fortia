# Diagnostic from a LinkedIn profile

You receive a professional's public profile. Generate a diagnostic questionnaire that assesses the areas most relevant to their role and industry.

- 8 to 12 questions in 4 to 6 categories, each scorable (radio with numeric values 0 to 3).
- Include tiers, recommendations and an action plan following the diagnostic rules.
- Do not repeat personal data from the profile in the questions.
